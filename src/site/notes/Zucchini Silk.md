* **Comments**: A savory, potassium-rich replacement for mashed potatoes.
* **Serving Notes**: 

### Equipment & Processing
* **Finishing**: [[Equipment/Magimix\|Magimix]]

---

### Ingredients and Method

| **Ingredient**        | **Weight**   | **Method**                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Zucchini** (Peeled) | **500g**     | **Peel** the zucchini completely (to ensure a white "potato" look). **Chop** into 2cm chunks.                                               |
| **Garlic Cloves**     | **2 pcs**    | **Steam** the zucchini and garlic until very soft (about 8–10 mins).                                                                        |
| **Almond Milk**       | **30g**      | **Drain** the zucchini in a colander for 2 mins. Do not skip this; you want to remove the excess "vegetal" water.                           |
| **Lemon Juice**       | **10g**      | **Transfer** to the [[Equipment/Magimix\|Magimix]] 4200XL with the S-blade. **Add** the almond milk, lemon juice, salt and plenty of black pepper.             |
| **Xanthan Gum**       | **0.5g**     | **Process** on high. While running, **dust in** the xanthan gum. This emulsifies the remaining water and fiber into a glossy, thick "Silk." |
| **Salt**              | **5g**       |                                                                                                                                             |
| **Black Pepper**      | **to taste** |                                                                                                                                             |
