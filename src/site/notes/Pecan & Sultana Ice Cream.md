* **Comments**: So good. SO good. Soak the sultanas in sherry if you want. It's worth it - it will stop the sultanas drawing moisture from the base, and if you add the soaking liquid to the base before churning it will lower the freeze point resulting in a smooooooooooth ice cream at lower temperatures. The high egg yolk content can easily stand up to the temperature drop.
* **Serving Notes**: 

---

### Ingredients and Method
| **Ingredients**             | **Quantity** | **Method**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Whole milk                  | 340 g        | Combine the brown sugar, egg yolks, salt, and ground cinnamon in a medium bowl.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Heavy cream                 | 285 g        |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Light brown sugar           | 170 g        | Whisk the milk and heavy cream into the egg mixture until combined.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Egg yolk                    | 115 g        |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Diamond Crystal kosher salt | 2 g          | Transfer the mixture to a saucier. Add the vanilla bean and cinnamon stick (if using).                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Ground cinnamon             | 1/4 tsp      |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Vanilla bean (optional)     | 1 unit       | Cook over medium-low heat until warm to the touch, then increase heat to medium. Stir and scrape constantly with a flexible spatula until the custard reaches **68°C**.                                                                                                      |
| Cinnamon stick (optional)   | 1 unit       |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Vanilla extract             | 15 g         | Pour the custard through a fine-mesh sieve into a large stainless steel bowl. Stir in the vanilla extract.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Pecans, toasted             | 30 g         | Cover and refrigerate to continue infusing overnight, or until the base is no warmer than **4°C**.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Sultanas                    | 45 g         | Remove the vanilla bean and cinnamon stick. If you soaked the sultanas in sherry, this is the moment to drain the 'tanas and add the sherry to the base. Churn in the ice cream machine according to the manufacturer’s directions until the custard looks fluffy and thick. |
|                             |              | Transfer to a container and fold in the toasted pecans and sultanas. Freeze until firm.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
