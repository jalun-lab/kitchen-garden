I am a person cursed with the combination of an enormous appetite, and the desire to not be a big fat fatty. 'Every Day Food' is my collection of the recipes I make to facilitate both of those requirements. 

They are generally high volume, high protein and low calorie density foods using a bit of sciencey type understanding and kitchen know-how to do wacky things like create chocolate pudding out of zucchini and protein powder. Are the [[Protein Puddings and Ice Creams\|Protein Puddings and Ice Creams]] as good as 'real' gelato? No. But they're still damn good! And they scratch the itch!

Some of these are complete meals, most are components or techniques. A lot of them are desserts.
