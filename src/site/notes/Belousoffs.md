***Served to:***  the Belousoffs
***On:***  7/3/26
### Smash Burgers

- **Brisket/Chuck/Short Rib Smash Burgers:** 110g coarse-ground patties on house-made, Nigella-seeded **Lepinja**.
    
- **Smoky Urnebes:** A whipped emulsion of Ki Sheep Cheese, roasted peppers, and smoke.
- **Aioli** - Mayonnaise and confit garlic
    
- **Compressed Shopska:** Vacuum-compressed cucumbers, tomatoes and peppers topped with a "Mountain" of **Ki Sheep Cheese Snow**.
    
- **Pečen Krumpir:** 85°C Sous Vide Sebago potatoes, scuffed and roasted in beef tallow.
    

### Pretentious Dessert

- **The Structure:** Buttered, "scrunched" Filo nests, baked until shatter-crisp.
    
- **The Filling:** **Tikvarnik Creme** (Smooth, piped pumpkin custard).
    
- **The Cold:** **Dark Malt Extract Ice Cream**
    
- **The Hot:** **Earl Grey & Mincemeat Gastrique** (A sharp, spiced, citrus-infused reduction).

### Technical Recipes

#### 1. Earl Grey Mincemeat Gastrique

1. **Caramel:** Melt 100g sugar to amber.
2. **Deglaze:** Add 80ml Apple Cider Vinegar and 50ml triple-strength Earl Grey tea.
3. **Simmer:** Add 150g Fruit Mincemeat. Reduce until syrupy.
#### 2. Tikvarnik Creme (The Filling)

1. **Bake:** 1kg SV Pumpkin, 150g sugar, 3 eggs, 200ml milk, 150g flour. Bake at 180°C until set.
2. **Transform:** Once cooled slightly, blitz in the **Magimix** with 50ml thick cream until velvet-smooth. Transfer to a piping bag.
#### 3. Smoky Urnebes

1. **Blitz:** 50/50 roasted red peppers and Ki Sheep Cheese.
2. **Infuse:** Place in a bowl, cover with wrap, and use the **Smoke Infuser** for 3 minutes.
