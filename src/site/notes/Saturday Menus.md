My new year's resolution for 2026, inspired by [this](https://amandalitman.substack.com/p/our-year-of-saturday-dinners?utm_source=substack&utm_medium=email), is to host a dinner every other Saturday that we're home. Because of the age of our kids, our friends kids, and the distance most of our guests will be travelling, I made it lunch instead.

So every other Saturday in 2026 I'll host a lunch and post the menu here, along with comments about what worked, what didn't, how easy the prep and cook was and how strong the response was.
